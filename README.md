The **smooth trackpad** gives you precise pointer control on your computer. Move your finger across the pad area and the cursor follows. Taps register as clicks, and dragging works the way you would expect, so you can move windows, select text, and browse the web without a mouse.

The **D-pad navigation** is built for menu-driven interfaces such as KDE Bigscreen. Up, down, left, and right directions let you move through menus, lists, and media libraries with clear, deliberate presses instead of guessing where a cursor will land.

**Media and volume controls** are grouped together so playback and sound are never more than one tap away. Play, pause, skip forward, skip back, and volume up and down all sit in easy thumb reach.

**Clipboard sync** lets you copy something on your phone and paste it on your computer, or the other way around. It is genuinely useful for sharing links, addresses, or short notes between devices without typing anything twice.

**A/C/V shortcuts** give you fast access to select all, copy, and paste directly from the remote, which speeds up text work enormously when you are not using a keyboard.

**Auto-repeat inputs** mean that holding a direction or volume button keeps the action going, just like holding a key on a physical keyboard. Scrolling through long lists or nudging the volume becomes effortless.

Finally, **light and dark themes** let you match the remote to your room and your preferences. The dark theme is especially kind to your eyes during evening viewing sessions.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

If the app does not open, double-check that you extracted the bundle fully before launching. Running an application from inside a compressed archive often fails. Right-click, choose Extract All, and launch from the unpacked folder instead.

If Windows shows a warning on first launch, that is normal for apps downloaded from the internet. Allow the app to run and it will open normally on subsequent launches.

If the remote does not seem to control your computer, make sure both devices are on the same home network and that the companion connection software on your computer is running. Restarting both the app and the computer-side software usually clears up any temporary hiccups. Keeping the app updated from the same download link ensures you have the latest fixes.
